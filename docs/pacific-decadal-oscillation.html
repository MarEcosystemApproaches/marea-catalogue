12 Pacific Decadal Oscillation

Data Type: Tabular Data

Spatial Scope: North Pacific

Duration 1854-2025

Source: NOAA ERSST, https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/monitoring/pdo/

Contact:

12.1 Introduction to Indicator

The Pacific Decadal Oscillation is a recurring pattern of sea surface temperature and air pressure variability in the Pacific Ocean, north of 20° latitude. Like the ENSO, the PDO varies on an interannual-to-interdecadal scale, and its pattern has broad affects on oceanographic and atmospheric processes in the North Pacific (Mantua et al. 1997).

12.3 Trends

As of the most recent data entry in 8/2025, the PDO value is -3.23, which is low among values in the timeseries. The PDO value is currently below the long-term mean, and has been since 12/2019. The PDO value has followed a(n) decreasing trend in recent years (Fig. 12.1).

12.4 Relevance to Research and Stock Assessments

The PDO affects several processes in the Pacific ocean and is linked to population dynamics of important harvested species such as salmon (Mantua et al. 1997). In the Canadian Atlantic, PDO is not expected to be a major driver of fisheries changes.
