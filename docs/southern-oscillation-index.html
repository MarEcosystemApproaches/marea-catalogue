11 Southern Oscillation Index

Data Type: Tabular Data

Spatial Scope: Equatorial Pacific

Duration 1951-2025

Source: NOAA CPC, https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/data/indices/soi

Contact:

11.1 Introduction to Indicator

The Southern Oscillation Index describes differences in sea level pressure in the Southern Pacific, between Tahiti and Darwin, Australia, normalized by by monthly standard deviations, and correlates with changes in ocean temperature in the South Pacific (Chen 1982).

Positive phases of the SOI describe lower-than-average pressure at Darwin and higher-than-average pressure at Tahiti, and negative phases describe the opposite. Negative phases are indicative of El Niño conditions including warm water in the Pacific, and positive phases of La Niña.

11.2 View Data 

library(plotly)

plotly_df <- data@data %>%
  mutate(date = lubridate::make_date(year, month, day = 1)) %>%
  mutate(color = ifelse(anomaly_value < 0,"#d63227","#3e5eb8"))

p <- plot_ly(plotly_df, x = ~date,y = ~anomaly_value) %>%
  add_bars(marker = list(color = ~color),
           hovertemplate = "Anomaly Value: %{y:.2f} <extra></extra>")  %>%
    layout(
    title = "Southern Oscillation Index for Equatorial Pacific",
    xaxis = list(title = "Date",
                 hoverformat = "%b %Y" ),
    yaxis = list(title = "SOI Index",
                 fixedrange = T),
    hovermode = "x unified",
    margin = list(t = 80)
    
  ) %>%
  config(displayModeBar = FALSE)

p

11.3 Trends

As of the most recent data entry in 8/2025, the SOI value is 0.7, which is moderate among values in the timeseries. The SOI value is currently above the long-term mean, and has been since 9/2024. The SOI value has followed a(n) increasing trend in recent years (Fig. 11.1).

11.4 Relevance to Research and Stock Assessments

The SOI is linked to several oceanographic processes in the Pacific and has several potential impacts on tropical Pacific fisheries (D. Harrison and Chiodi 2015). However, it is not expected to be a major driver of fisheries in the Canadian Atlantic.
